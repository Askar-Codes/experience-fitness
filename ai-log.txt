AI LOG — Experience Fitness website
Author: Balakhazy Askar

Assignment 1: I used AI  to help write some of the body text on the site,
since English is not my first language. All real facts (prices, address, hours)
came from me, not the AI.

Assignment 2: I used AI to talk through layout and color ideas — for example,
picking a palette based on photos of the actual gym, and deciding where flexbox
made more sense than grid. I also used it to help debug a real CSS specificity
bug I ran into (a table getting hidden behind another block because of a rule
priority conflict), and to figure out why my bento grid math was off after I
added a heading inside the grid container.

I wrote the HTML and CSS myself. AI was used for wording, ideas, and debugging
help, not for generating the site's structure or styling on its own.
